about

    Rachel Neches is the Senior Data & Policy Researcher at the Center for an Urban Future, an independent think thank focused on building a stronger and more equitable New York City.

    Rachel's work translates data, research, and interviews into actionable policy insights that inform decision-making across the city and state. Her research focuses on a range of issues from economic mobility to workforce development to the creative economy and older adults.

    In 2025, Rachel was a Forefront Fellow with the Urban Design Forum, where she worked alongside a diverse cohort to envision a New York City where older adults can thrive with support and dignity.

    She graduated with a BA in Urban Studies (Economics) from Barnard College. Before joining the Center, she served on the Policy, Planning & Research team at the Times Square Alliance, where she leveraged data and advocacy to support the area's recovery through the pandemic.

    Her research been referenced in numerous publications, including the New York Times, WNYC, Crain's New York Business, City Limits, the New York Post, and more.

    She lives in Brooklyn.
